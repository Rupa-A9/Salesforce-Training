# **DAY 15 – Data Management, Data Quality, Migration & Enterprise Governance**

---

## **Summary**

The goal of Day 15 was to understand how enterprise systems depend on structured data management practices to ensure accuracy, consistency, and reliability across the organization.

This day focused on how data is imported, exported, migrated, and maintained in Salesforce, and how poor data quality directly impacts business decisions, automation, and reporting.

The learning emphasized how enterprises handle large-scale data operations using tools like Data Import Wizard, Data Loader, Data Export Service, validation rules, duplicate management, and governance frameworks to maintain trustworthy systems.

---

## **Topics Covered**

---

## **1. Data Management Fundamentals**

### **What is Data Management?**

Data management refers to the structured process of collecting, storing, organizing, maintaining, and governing enterprise data throughout its lifecycle.

Instead of random data handling, enterprises follow defined standards to ensure data remains usable and reliable.

### **Core Areas of Data Management**

* Data import and export processes
* Data migration between systems
* Bulk data operations
* Data lifecycle control
* Data maintenance strategies

### **Importance in Enterprise Systems**

* Ensures consistency across departments
* Supports accurate reporting and analytics
* Reduces operational errors
* Improves decision-making speed
* Enables system scalability

---

## **2. Data Import and Export**

### **Data Import**

Data import is the process of bringing external data into Salesforce from sources like CSV files or legacy systems.

### **Tools Used**

* Data Import Wizard (small to medium datasets)
* Data Loader (large-scale or complex imports)

### **Data Export**

Data export is the process of extracting Salesforce data for backup, reporting, or system integration.

### **Tools Used**

* Data Export Service
* Data Loader

### **Enterprise Use Cases**

* Migrating from Excel or legacy CRM systems
* System backup and disaster recovery
* Integrating external applications
* Bulk updates to records

---

## **3. Data Migration Challenges**

### **What is Data Migration?**

Data migration is the process of moving data from one system to another while maintaining structure, accuracy, and consistency.

### **Key Challenges**

* Duplicate records across systems
* Missing or incomplete fields
* Inconsistent formatting standards
* Incorrect field mapping
* Invalid or outdated data

### **Business Impact**

* Incorrect dashboards and reports
* Broken automation workflows
* Poor customer experience
* Loss of trust in CRM system

---

## **4. Duplicate Prevention**

### **What are Duplicate Records?**

Duplicate records occur when the same real-world entity exists multiple times in Salesforce.

### **Causes of Duplicates**

* Multiple data entry points
* Lack of validation rules
* Manual errors
* Importing unclean external data

### **Prevention Methods**

* Matching Rules
* Duplicate Rules
* Validation Rules
* Standardized data entry formats

### **Business Risks**

* Confusion in customer identity
* Duplicate communication
* Wasted sales efforts
* Inaccurate reporting and forecasting

---

## **5. Data Quality in Enterprises**

### **Dimensions of Data Quality**

* Accuracy
* Completeness
* Consistency
* Timeliness
* Uniqueness
* Validity

### **Why Data Quality Matters**

High-quality data ensures that business operations, analytics, and automation run correctly without errors or inconsistencies.

### **Impact of Poor Data**

* Revenue loss
* Operational inefficiency
* Wrong business decisions
* Poor customer experience
* Failed automation processes

---

## **6. Enterprise Governance in Data Management**

### **What is Data Governance?**

Data governance defines the rules, roles, and responsibilities for managing enterprise data securely and consistently.

### **Key Components**

* Data ownership structure
* Access control policies
* Security and compliance rules
* Data monitoring systems
* Quality enforcement standards

### **Why Governance is Important**

* Prevents unauthorized data changes
* Ensures compliance with regulations
* Maintains data integrity
* Improves accountability
* Protects sensitive business information

## **CORE TASKS**

---

## **1. Bad Data Scenarios**

### **Examples of Data Issues**

* Duplicate customer records
* Missing email or phone numbers
* Incorrect or inconsistent department values
* Invalid attendance or status fields
* Outdated contact information
* Missing required fields in records
* Inconsistent naming conventions
* Incorrect formatting of dates or currency
* Unverified or fake entries
* Stale records not updated for months

### **Business Problems Caused**

* Incorrect reporting and forecasting
* Failed automation processes
* Poor customer communication
* Inefficient sales operations
* Loss of revenue opportunities

---

## **2. Data Migration Thinking**

### **Scenario: Excel to Salesforce Migration**

### **Challenges During Migration**

* Duplicate records in spreadsheets
* Missing mandatory fields
* Inconsistent formats (date, currency, phone)
* Incorrect mapping between fields
* Unclean or unverified source data

### **Business Impact**

* Data corruption in CRM system
* Broken dashboards and reports
* Reduced user trust in Salesforce
* Automation failures in workflows

---

## **3. Data Governance Reflection**

Clean and reliable data is critical for enterprise systems because every business decision, report, and automation depends on it.

Without proper governance, organizations face risks such as incorrect decisions, compliance issues, operational failures, and loss of data integrity.

Governance ensures that data remains accurate, secure, consistent, and usable across all departments.

---

## **4. Enterprise Thinking Scenario**

### **Problem: 50,000 Incorrect Records Imported**

### **Possible Consequences**

* Wrong customer notifications
* Incorrect financial or attendance records
* Failed reports and dashboards
* Broken automation workflows
* High-level decision-making errors
* Loss of trust in system data

### **Conclusion**

Large-scale data mistakes multiply business risk and can disrupt entire enterprise operations if not controlled through validation and governance.
